Overview
This document covers the most frequently reported technical errors across
company platforms and internal tools, along with their causes and
recommended fixes. These are general platform-level issues, distinct from
account access or permission requests, which are covered in separate
documents.

Error: "Session Expired"
Cause: Your login session automatically times out after 30 minutes of
inactivity, as a standard security measure across all internal web
applications.
Fix: Simply log in again. No further action is needed, and no data is
typically lost if you were in the middle of filling out a form, since most
internal tools auto-save drafts periodically.
Prevention: If you frequently work in long stretches without interacting
with the browser tab, consider enabling the "Keep me signed in" option
where available, which extends the timeout window to 4 hours.

Error: "403 Forbidden - Insufficient Permissions"
Cause: Your account role does not include the permissions required to
access this specific feature, page, or dataset.
Fix: This is an access-level issue, not a technical bug. Request the
correct role or access level through the appropriate access request
process (see Jira Access Request Process or VPN Access Request Guide,
depending on which system is involved).
Note: If you previously had access to this exact feature and it recently
stopped working, this may indicate a recent permissions change during a
security review; contact IT Helpdesk to confirm whether this was
intentional.

Error: "Unable to Sync Data" / "Sync Failed"
Cause: Usually caused by a temporary network interruption, a brief server-
side maintenance window, or a large dataset taking longer than the
expected sync window.
Fix: Wait 5 minutes and retry the sync manually using the "Retry Sync"
button available in most tools experiencing this error. If the issue
persists for over 30 minutes continuously, this likely indicates a
platform-wide issue rather than something specific to your account;
escalate to platform support.
Additional check: Confirm your internet connection is stable by loading
another website; if other sites also fail to load, the issue is local to
your network, not the platform.

Error: "500 Internal Server Error"
Cause: An unexpected server-side error, often temporary and related to
backend processing rather than anything you did incorrectly.
Fix: Refresh the page and retry the action after a minute. If the error
repeats on the exact same action (e.g., always fails when submitting a
specific form), note the exact steps that trigger it and report to
platform support, as this may indicate a genuine bug rather than a
transient issue.

Error: "Page Not Loading" / Blank White Screen
Cause: Most commonly a browser caching issue, an outdated browser version,
or a temporary content delivery network (CDN) issue on the platform side.
Fix:
1. Hard refresh the page (Ctrl+Shift+R on Windows/Linux, Cmd+Shift+R on
   Mac) to bypass cached files.
2. Clear browser cache and cookies for the site specifically.
3. Try accessing the page in an incognito/private browsing window to rule
   out browser extensions interfering.
4. If the issue persists across multiple browsers and after clearing
   cache, this indicates a platform-side issue; escalate to platform
   support.

Error: "File Upload Failed"
Cause: Commonly caused by file size exceeding the platform's upload limit
(typically 25 MB for most internal tools), an unsupported file format, or
a network interruption during upload.
Fix: Confirm the file is under the size limit and in a supported format
(check the specific tool's documentation for accepted formats, typically
PDF, DOCX, XLSX, PNG, and JPG). For large files, ensure a stable network
connection, ideally wired rather than WiFi, before retrying.

Error: Slow Performance / Pages Taking a Long Time to Load
Cause: Can result from high platform traffic during peak hours (typically
9-11 AM), a large volume of data being rendered on a single page, or local
device resource constraints (too many browser tabs or applications open).
Fix: Close unnecessary browser tabs and applications to free up system
resources. If slowness is specific to peak hours across the whole company,
this is often a known and temporary condition; check the internal status
page for any active performance advisories.

Error: "Invalid Token" or "Authentication Error" on API-connected tools
Cause: Your session token has expired or become invalid, often after a
password change or prolonged inactivity.
Fix: Log out completely and log back in to refresh your authentication
token. If using an integration or connected app, you may need to
reauthorize the connection separately from your main account login.

When to Escalate vs. Self-Resolve
As a general guideline:
- Errors that resolve after a simple retry, refresh, or re-login do not
  need escalation.
- Errors that persist for more than 30 minutes, affect multiple users
  simultaneously, or block critical work should be escalated to platform
  support immediately rather than repeatedly retried.
- Always check the internal status page first during a suspected
  widespread outage, as many incidents are already being tracked and
  communicated company-wide before individual tickets are needed.

Contact and Escalation
For persistent or unresolved technical errors, contact platform support at
platformsupport@adp.com or through the IT Service Portal under
"Report an Issue". Include the exact error message, the time it occurred,
and the steps taken so far, as this significantly speeds up diagnosis.
