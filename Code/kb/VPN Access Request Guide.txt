Overview
Virtual Private Network (VPN) access allows employees to securely connect to
internal company systems, file shares, and applications when working from a
remote location, a client site, or a home office. VPN access is mandatory for
any employee who needs to reach internal-only resources such as the internal
wiki, internal build servers, shared network drives, or on-premise databases
while outside the corporate office network.

Who Needs VPN Access
- Employees working remotely on a regular or occasional basis
- Employees traveling for client meetings or conferences
- Contractors and vendors who require temporary access to internal systems
- Employees connecting from a home office during hybrid work arrangements

VPN access is not required for accessing cloud-based tools such as email,
calendar, or SaaS applications that are already internet-accessible; it is
specifically needed for internal, on-premise resources.

How to Request VPN Access
1. Log in to the IT Service Portal at portal.company.com using your company
   credentials.
2. Navigate to "Access Requests" and select "VPN Access" from the list of
   available request types.
3. Fill out the request form, including:
   - Your employee ID
   - Your department
   - Your reporting manager's name
   - Business justification for remote access (e.g., "hybrid work
     arrangement", "upcoming travel for client project")
4. Submit the request. Your manager will automatically receive an approval
   notification via email.
5. Once your manager approves the request, it is routed to the IT Security
   team for a final review, which typically takes an additional 4 to 8
   business hours.
6. Approval time overall: 1-2 business days under normal circumstances.

What Happens After Approval
Once approved, you will receive an email from itaccess@company.com containing:
- A link to download the approved VPN client software (GlobalProtect or
  Cisco AnyConnect, depending on your device type)
- Your unique VPN username (usually the same as your email prefix)
- A temporary activation PIN, valid for 48 hours, which you will use the
  first time you connect
- A link to the VPN Setup Guide with screenshots for Windows, Mac, and Linux

Setting Up the VPN Client
1. Download and install the VPN client using the link provided in your
   approval email.
2. Open the VPN client and enter the internal gateway address:
   vpn.company.com
3. When prompted, enter your company username and the temporary activation
   PIN from your approval email.
4. You will be prompted to set a permanent VPN password on first login.
5. After setup, the VPN client will prompt for multi-factor authentication
   (MFA) on every connection, using the Authenticator app already installed
   during onboarding.

Renewing or Extending VPN Access
VPN access is granted for a 12-month period by default and must be renewed
annually. You will receive an automatic reminder email 2 weeks before your
access expires. To renew, simply log a new request in the IT Service Portal
using the "VPN Renewal" request type, which usually processes faster than a
new request since your manager and business justification are already on
file.

Troubleshooting Common VPN Issues

Issue: "Unable to connect to VPN gateway"
Cause: Usually a temporary network issue or an outdated VPN client version.
Fix: Confirm your internet connection is stable, then check for a client
update within the VPN application settings. If the issue persists after
updating, restart your device and try again.

Issue: "Authentication failed" during VPN login
Cause: Incorrect password, or your MFA app is out of sync.
Fix: Reset your VPN password using the "Forgot VPN Password" link in the
VPN client login screen. If MFA codes are being rejected, check that your
phone's date and time are set to automatic/network time, as MFA codes are
time-sensitive.

Issue: VPN connects but internal sites are still unreachable
Cause: Split-tunneling configuration issue, or the internal DNS server is
not being applied correctly.
Fix: Disconnect and reconnect the VPN client. If the issue persists,
contact IT Helpdesk to have your VPN profile reset.

Revoking VPN Access
VPN access is automatically revoked when:
- An employee's role no longer requires remote access, as flagged during
  quarterly access reviews
- An employee leaves the company (handled automatically as part of the
  offboarding process)
- VPN access has not been used for 90 consecutive days (inactive access is
  automatically suspended for security purposes, and can be reactivated
  upon request)
