Overview
This checklist outlines everything a new employee should complete during
their first week to ensure smooth access to all required tools and systems.
Most items are partially automated, but several steps require the new
employee or their manager to take direct action.

Day 1: Automatic Setup (No Action Needed)
The following are provisioned automatically by IT before your start date,
based on the information submitted by HR during the hiring process:
- Company email account and inbox
- Slack account, added to your team's default channels
- Access to the HR self-service portal for payroll and benefits
- A company laptop, pre-configured with standard software (browser,
  office suite, security agent, and VPN client pre-installed but not yet
  activated)

Day 1-2: Required Employee Actions
1. Complete your account activation by logging into login.company.com
   using the temporary credentials provided in your welcome email, and
   immediately set a permanent password following the password policy
   (see Password Reset Instructions document).
2. Set up multi-factor authentication (MFA) using the Authenticator app,
   following the guided setup link sent to your company email on Day 1.
3. Review and acknowledge the Employee Handbook and IT Acceptable Use
   Policy through the HR portal; this is a mandatory compliance step.

Week 1: Requesting Tool Access
Unlike email and Slack, most project-specific tools are not provisioned
automatically and must be requested individually based on your role:
1. Jira Access: Submit a request via the IT Service Portal specifying your
   project and required role (see Jira Access Request Process document).
2. Confluence Access: Typically granted alongside Jira access for the same
   project; specify this in the same request if needed.
3. GitHub/Source Control Access: Submit a separate request under
   "Software Access" > "GitHub Access", specifying the repository or
   organization team you need to join.
4. VPN Access: Required only if you will be working remotely or need
   access to internal-only systems (see VPN Access Request Guide). Not
   needed for employees working exclusively from the office using
   internal network connections.

Week 1: Mandatory Training
1. Complete the mandatory Security Awareness Training module, assigned
   automatically and visible in your HR portal dashboard. This must be
   completed within your first 5 business days.
2. Complete any role-specific compliance training assigned by your
   department (e.g., data privacy training for roles handling customer
   data).

Week 1: Manager Coordination
New employees should schedule a 1:1 meeting with their direct manager
during the first week to cover:
- Role-specific access needs beyond the standard tools listed above
  (e.g., access to specific internal dashboards, client systems, or
  department-specific software)
- Introduction to key team members and points of contact
- Review of immediate priorities and expectations for the first 30 days

Software Installation Requests
If your role requires software beyond what is pre-installed on your
company laptop (for example, specialized development tools, design
software, or licensed applications), submit a request via the IT Service
Portal under "Software Installation" (see Requesting New Software
Installation document for the full process).

Common Onboarding Issues

Issue: Welcome email with temporary credentials never arrived
Cause: Email provisioning delay, or incorrect personal email on file with
HR (welcome emails are sent to your personal email before your company
account exists).
Fix: Contact your HR representative to confirm your personal email on
file and have the welcome email resent.

Issue: Laptop received but cannot log in with temporary credentials
Cause: Account provisioning may not have completed before laptop shipment,
which can occasionally happen with tight start-date timelines.
Fix: Contact IT Helpdesk directly; they can verify account status and
issue temporary credentials over a verified call if the automated email
did not arrive.

Issue: Slack account exists but missing from expected team channels
Cause: Channel membership beyond default company-wide channels is managed
by team leads, not automatically provisioned.
Fix: Ask your manager or a teammate to add you to the relevant
team-specific channels.

30-60-90 Day Access Review
At the 30-day mark, managers are expected to review whether their new team
member has all necessary access and flag any gaps to IT. A more formal
access audit occurs at 90 days as part of standard company security
practice, at which point unused or unnecessary access grants may be
reviewed and cleaned up.

Contact and Escalation
For any onboarding-related access issue blocking your ability to start
work, contact the IT Helpdesk at ithelpdesk@adp.com or extension 7075949430.
New employees experiencing Day 1 issues should also loop in their manager,
as some onboarding steps require manager-level approval to resolve
quickly.
