Overview
This document covers how to reset your company account password, what the
password policy requires, and how to resolve common issues encountered
during the reset process. Your company account password is used across
email, the IT Service Portal, VPN (initial setup only), and most internal
web applications through single sign-on (SSO).

Standard Password Reset Process
1. Go to the company login page at login.company.com and click
   "Forgot Password" beneath the login form.
2. Enter your registered company email address exactly as it appears in
   your employee profile.
3. Check your email inbox for a password reset link. The email will arrive
   from noreply-security@company.com within a few minutes.
4. The reset link is valid for 30 minutes from the time it is sent. If it
   expires before you use it, simply repeat step 1 to request a new one.
5. Click the link and enter your new password twice to confirm it.
6. You will be redirected to the login page to sign in with your new
   password.

Password Policy Requirements
All company account passwords must meet the following criteria:
- Minimum length of 12 characters
- At least one uppercase letter (A-Z)
- At least one lowercase letter (a-z)
- At least one numeric digit (0-9)
- At least one special character (e.g., !, @, #, $, %, &, *)
- Cannot reuse any of your last 5 previous passwords
- Cannot contain your username, first name, or last name

Passwords expire every 90 days. You will receive an automated reminder
email 7 days before expiration, prompting you to change your password
proactively before it locks you out.

If You Don't Receive the Reset Email
1. Check your spam or junk folder, as automated security emails are
   sometimes filtered incorrectly by email clients.
2. Confirm you entered the correct registered email address; using a
   personal email address instead of your company email will not work,
   as the system only recognizes registered company addresses.
3. Wait a full 10 minutes, as email delivery can occasionally be delayed
   during high system load.
4. If still not received after 10 minutes, contact the IT Helpdesk to have
   a reset link manually triggered from the admin console.

Account Lockout After Failed Attempts
For security purposes, your account will be temporarily locked after 5
consecutive failed login attempts. This lockout lasts 15 minutes and
resets automatically. If you need immediate access before the lockout
period ends, contact the IT Helpdesk to have the lockout manually cleared
after identity verification.

Changing Your Password Without a Reset (While Logged In)
If you already know your current password but want to change it
proactively (for example, ahead of the 90-day expiration), you do not
need to use the "Forgot Password" flow:
1. Log in to the IT Service Portal.
2. Navigate to "My Account" > "Security Settings" > "Change Password".
3. Enter your current password, then your new password twice.
4. Save changes. You will be logged out of all active sessions and must
   log back in with the new password.

Two-Factor / Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
Password resets do not affect your MFA setup. If you are also having
trouble with MFA codes (for example, your authenticator app was reset
after getting a new phone), this requires a separate MFA reset request,
submitted through the IT Service Portal under "Security" > "MFA Reset",
which requires manager approval due to the sensitivity of bypassing a
second authentication factor.

Common Password Reset Issues

Issue: New password is rejected as "does not meet policy requirements"
Cause: Missing one of the required character types, or reusing a recent
password.
Fix: Review the password policy list above carefully; the system will
usually indicate which specific rule was not met.

Issue: Reset link says "invalid or expired" immediately after receiving it
Cause: The link may have already been used once, or more than 30 minutes
passed since it was generated.
Fix: Request a fresh reset link; each link is single-use only.

Issue: Successfully reset password but still cannot log in
Cause: Browser cache storing an old session, or the change has not yet
propagated across all connected systems (can take up to 5 minutes for
SSO-connected apps).
Fix: Clear browser cache or try an incognito/private window. Wait a few
minutes before retrying on SSO-connected applications.
